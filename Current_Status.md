# Current Status

*Where the thinking actually stands. Updated in the register of work-in-progress, not as settled record.*

## State of the essays

Five drafts, all at v0.14, all thinking-in-progress:

- **1a — The Lion and the Deer.** The biological ground. Gates welded to capability; effort and risk as two currencies of one gate; position in the chain sets which gate applies and the character of failure. Solid as setup. Minor: the clean graded/terminal binary is complicated by its own aging-lion (graded-then-terminal), which the draft half-notes.
- **1b — The Emergent Order and the Point-Line Axiom.** The engine chapter. The point-to-line move, the two natural experiments (rebound, death). Carries the centre of gravity. Leans on the height data harder than the data alone supports (see open edges) and walls off the "medium beneath the medium."
- **1c — The Agrarian World.** The concrete world on its own terms — storability, transport, variability, insurance, the premium. Built deliberately without the axiom, so the axiom is earned rather than assumed. Ends on the free-rider question it cannot answer from its own terms.
- **1d — The Configurations.** One line-function, four housings, ground selects. Egypt / China / India / Abrahamic. The Abrahamic case is the one derived backward from observed portability rather than forward from ground.
- **1e — The Vertical Engine.** The Andes as test. Holographic vs additive information; the Inca centralising permission, not calories. The strongest single mechanism in the set — and the chapter whose success partly dismantles 1d's thesis (below).

## The core as it stands this session

The four ideas are stated in the README and will not be repeated here. What matters for status is that the distillation has moved past the drafts in specific ways, and past earlier versions of the distillation too. The current core is: *pressure and its address are the invariant; the enlarged self is the engine that pays it; the variation is the same selective process meeting different ground, not a second force; nothing is ranked; the individual-housed answer is the basin of last resort, not a summit.* And underneath all of it: one Darwinian process on two substrates, with non-human agriculture (ant, bee) as the control case that makes the human answer legible as evolution.

## What shifted this session

Several corrections were earned that the drafts do not yet carry. Recording them because they are the live edge, and because at least some belong back in the essays.

**The causal order flipped.** The drafts imply the inward medium (language operated inward, carrying a law whole in one mind) is what *lets* the individual-housed core form — a capacity India had and the Andes lacked. The corrected reading reverses it: the foreclosure of every other option is the cause; the inward medium is what gets elaborated under the load once things have already been forced into the individual. Response, not precondition. This flip should probably reach 1d and 1e.

**"Deepest inward" lost its achievement-flavour.** India housing the line furthest inside is not the far, deepest end of an axis of accomplishment. It is most-cornered. Maximum internalisation is maximum lack of external option, read from the inside. The drafts still carry the faint gradient of depth-as-attainment ("housed furthest," "deepest internalisation"); it wants stripping.

**The "switch" dissolved.** An earlier line of this session hunted for what throws the fork between holographic and additive — a bistable-switch story, a hidden variable. That was an error: it treated the individual-core as a destination something reaches for. There is no destination. There is descent through whatever the local mix leaves open, and no hand behind the variation. The seat is not unoccupied-because-deep; it was mis-drawn. This retired the "unoccupied centre / claim the material is asking you to make next" framing from earlier in the session — that framing was itself the completeness-demand in disguise.

**The map is not owed.** A related correction: asking the material to *map* how geography/time/neighbourhood open and close basins is the perfection-frame wearing a methodological coat. If the driver is seen, the map is redundant; if it isn't, the map is a hope that accumulation will cough up a driver. Map and driver are inversely related. What is owed is not coverage but stress — the breaking case.

**Evolution moved to the core, and did real work.** Putting selection at the centre (rather than as backdrop) closed the soft seam without romance, turned the four configurations into adaptive radiation (making the anti-ranking discipline *forced by the framing* rather than a tone-correction repeatedly reapplied), and reframed "undriven variation" into its truer form: one driver, local outputs. The ant/bee comparison is not an aside to add — it is the control case that makes the whole thing legible as evolution rather than as human exceptionalism.

## Open edges

**The soft seam (partly bridged).** Why a stored-grain problem gets answered by a change in what a person feels themselves to be. Evolution-at-the-core bridges it in principle; the bridge holds only as strongly as the next edge.

**The unbuilt plank (now load-bearing).** Whether the cultural case genuinely meets the Darwinian conditions — heritable variation, replicating units, fidelity enough to accumulate. Settled for ants, asserted for cultures. This is the "medium beneath the medium" the drafts wall off. Once evolution is the core, the core rests here, and the wall has to come down.

**The empirical keystone.** The agrarian height decline carries a lot (two-ness in 1b, premium-in-bone in 1c). The population-grew move rules out "base failing" but not the flatter rival — diet simply got narrower and denser-living sicker, calories that were never there rather than deferred into a reserve. Bone height alone cannot show the missing centimetres were *banked*. Either soften or find independent evidence of the banking.

**The interaction / re-cooking layer.** Settled answers meet, compete, absorb, hybridise, break off portable cores, arise at each other's failure points (Buddhism carried out of India; the Abrahamic frameworks forming in the gap a failing system left). The drafts treat this as footnote. It may be a second cooking as large as the first, and it is the part currently most under-built.

**1e vs 1d, unresolved in the drafts.** 1e shows friction does not sort the house (India and the Andes share it, diverge), which means 1d's "ground selects the house" is weaker at the end of Part One than it looks in 1d, and its real selector is non-geographic. The current distillation absorbs this (variation as local settling, no ranked destinations), but the *essays* still narrate 1e as clean vindication. The tone in 1e wants matching to the actual state.

## The one live task

Not completing anything. Find the case most likely to break the core — *pressure and its address, variation as one process meeting different ground* — the way the Andes broke friction. If it survives, no map is needed and none would help. If it breaks, there is a deeper driver, and it will have been found the only way any of this got found: by looking to kill the thing, not to finish it.
